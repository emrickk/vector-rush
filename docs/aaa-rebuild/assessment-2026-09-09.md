# Vector Rush quality assessment

The current game does not meet the requested AAA standard. Independent visual inspection rejects the direct-production environment as a substantial regression from the preserved original. Rebuild from the original spatial identity; do not polish or replicate the rejected environment.

## What the images establish

The image-capable Sol critic inspected six approved original/native/target images. [Independent visual report](critic-002.md) records the findings and seven native acceptance gates. The parent could not view images directly and attributes appearance findings to this independent inspection.

| Area | Finding | Required action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Opening city | The replacement's huge left slab, repetitive right block and flat blue-gray lighting erase the elevated road's skyline, infrastructure layers and altitude cues. | Preserve the original elevated curve and city layers; replace weak foreground construction and improve grounded local lighting. Two new facades cannot solve the whole opening. |
| Warm gallery | The replacement removes the original enclosure, warm fixture rhythm and framed cool exit, leaving an open scaffold. | Retain the original enclosed profile and rebuild ribs, panels, fixtures and material/light response together. |
| Original finish | The original remains stronger, but dark voids, crude prominent structures, flat panels and weak road response fall short of the concept target. | Treat the original as a composition control, not accepted AAA art. |
| Craft and HUD | The critic finds them legible in the reviewed pairs. | Retain as controls for the gallery experiment; this is not a whole-game craft/VFX/UI quality pass. |

No actual Wipeout Omega Collection reference was inspected. The supplied generated concepts support direction and composition only. A direct claim of parity with Wipeout would be unsupported.

[Test result](../../evidence/aaa-rebuild/resume-02/tests-03.xml) and [source identity](../../evidence/aaa-rebuild/resume-02/test-03-source-identity.json) identify this check. [Independent gameplay review](gameplay-critic-002.md) finds the original five production correction paths closed, with two remaining P2 issues:

- The public ledger convenience API can finalize a deadline before receiving an exact-tie crossing. The current director uses the correct ordered path; this is a public API regression risk.
- Settings can say a binding was saved or defaults restored after persistence failed. Success messages need to reflect disk-write results.

Earlier three-race evidence establishes repeatable automated completion on an older build. It is not current-build proof of human handling, fair competition, recovery in traffic or physical controller behavior. Native menu focus/activation, mixed automation record eligibility and filesystem failure handling remain required checks.

The actual Blender package import failed before publication. Its byte-level course hash differs from the current export. This is a serialization identity incompatibility, not evidence of a changed route. Preserve the strict rejection until the identity contract or package export is corrected and import reruns successfully. Do not simply remove the guard.

[Independent importer review](import-critic-001.md) also found discarded runtime course identity, optional validation after required resource publication, and a replacement hazard around ancestor colliders. The [corrective implementation](import-fixes.md) adds runtime identity checks, required-resource validation and ancestor-collider protection. Its four contract tests pass in the 114-test run. Actual corrected import, player build and native placement/rendering have not passed. The source-only review builder has not been executed.

## Committed production direction

First build one integrated warm-gallery section on the preserved original world. Keep track, camera, craft, HUD, cyan edge, exit and continuous backing shell. Author connected portal/ribs/panels with believable fixture housings, distinguish their materials, integrate local warm illumination and controlled road response, then inspect matched native off/on views and an uninterrupted traversal.

The independent critic's seven gates are authoritative for that experiment: comparable evidence, enclosure/assembly, material separation, lighting integration, route/craft readability, seamless section transition and visible concept-direction gains. A rejection requires a substantive correction to the cited defect. Export success, extra objects and small parameter changes do not earn adoption.

After the gallery earns adoption, expand the same construction/material/light standard across the circuit, with a separate city composition solution for the opening. In parallel, resolve the two gameplay findings and validate physics/AI/recovery, craft/VFX, interface/accessibility, sound and music. Finish with whole-lap native appearance, continuously observed motion/audio, human racing and isolated current-build performance. These requirements remain part of the full rebuild; the gallery is the first production gate, not a reduced final scope.
