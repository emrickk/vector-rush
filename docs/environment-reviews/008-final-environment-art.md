## Remaining limits and delivery decision

**The road is not certified clean.** Broad diagonal tonal bands remain in [219](../../evidence/environment-candidate-03/frames/frame-0219.png), [220](../../evidence/environment-candidate-03/frames/frame-0220.png) and the reveal anchor. Their cause and behavior over time are not established by these images. Retain the satin surface as a deliberate material redesign; preserve [the unresolved diagnosis](003-road-normal-shadow-controls.md). Do not describe the original sheen problem as solved or the final surface as artifact-free.

**The small timing-label crossing remains.** At frame 220 the exterior lamp diffuser overlaps `BEST LAP` and reduces its contrast. The large race-time digits remain readable. This is the existing localized limitation from review 006; it is not a reason for another HUD redesign. Its duration and distraction still need normal playback assessment.

**The environment's main quality ceiling is now identity and scale.** The gallery is constructed and the nearest glazing is more credible, but repeated skyline patterns, sparse ground/service relationships and the modest station silhouette keep the district from feeling distinctive. A later art pass should solve those larger relationships before multiplying detail or light count. The current corrections do not justify copying the same composition around the whole circuit.

No additional rebuild is requested on the strength of the inspected corrections. Deliver the frozen native benchmark and a current full-lap preview for user playback, with this review's limits intact. Assess continuous ordinary and boosted travel, nearby craft, supported frame shapes and current frame cost before broader expansion. A complete exported video is a review artifact; its existence is not a completed motion review.
