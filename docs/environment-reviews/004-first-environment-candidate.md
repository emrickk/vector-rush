# Environment review 004: first native candidate

Independent review, 2026-09-08. **Reject candidate 01 for benchmark acceptance and full-circuit expansion. Keep the gallery construction direction.** The new portal, ribs and ceiling are a clear step forward, but the city composition does not yet deliver the connected approach-to-landmark passage defined in [review 001](001-benchmark-direction.md). The strongest failure is the final reveal: the intended transit landmark is not recognizable, while a blank blue wall and an almost unlit tower become the main nearby objects.

## Evidence and limits

Directly inspected all five 1920 × 1080 candidate anchors and all five corresponding [locked baseline anchors](../../evidence/environment-baseline-01/baseline-manifest.json). The [candidate report](../../evidence/environment-candidate-01/environment-evidence.json) records 264 frames and five anchors, captured from 08:21:28 to 08:22:06 UTC. It explicitly reports `matchedWithinTolerance: false`: camera positions differ from the baseline by 0.221–0.597 m, with small angle and FOV differences. These images support composition judgments about large forms; they are not exact camera matches and must not be presented as a controlled pixel comparison.

The recording uses automated steering with normal physics at 24 simulation frames per second. The five anchors are grounded; only the final reveal anchor is boosting. **This report reviews still images.** I did not watch the complete video at normal speed, hear audio, inspect a crowded passage, test other aspect ratios, or measure performance. The presence of 264 recorded frames does not supply those acceptance checks.

## Three bounded corrections for the next candidate

1. **Compose one transit landmark across both exit and reveal.** Adjust placement, orientation and silhouette using the actual cameras for 04 and 05 together. It must first read through the exit frame, then remain the same recognizable structure in the final view. Give it an intelligible base, occupied or serviced middle, and a distinct roof or structural profile, separated from the far skyline by a deliberate gap. A sign is insufficient. Do not improve one anchor by moving the object out of the other. Acceptance: a viewer can point to the same transit structure in both images without being told its coordinates or name.

2. **Make the nearest city masses readable before adding detail elsewhere.** Correct the blue wall facing the camera in 05 through orientation, setbacks, openings and visible service-scale structure. Reveal the right tower's mass divisions with restrained surface light and selected floor groups; retain quiet areas rather than replacing darkness with a uniform window grid. Give the approach one clearly composed terrace/service grouping with a visible support or ground relationship. Use fewer strong nearby forms and quieter distant groups. Acceptance: the near objects read as buildings with a purpose and size, and the near, middle and far layers remain distinguishable without labels.

3. **Keep the gallery shell and improve its light hierarchy.** Reduce the upper-wall hotspot peaks and distribute enough softer light to keep the ceiling, ribs and joints readable. Protect the exit as the forward focal point. Give a small number of middle spans a distinct service recess, access condition or integrated channel, especially on the right; the remaining panels can stay quiet. Keep the entry portal, interior span and exit frame visibly related but distinct. Recheck the upper-right spill against the existing timing display at 04. Acceptance: construction remains readable without a row of equally dominant light patches, and the interior has one or two functional cues beyond repeated cladding.

These corrections should remain within the current passage and existing camera. More skyline population, more glowing trim, another HUD redesign or a global exposure lift would not address the composition failures documented here.

## Gate after correction

Capture the same five anchors and retain the actual pose-drift report. Judge their composition before expanding the kit. Then independently watch the complete corrected passage at normal playback speed for lamp strobing, window shimmer, road instability, disappearing geometry and loss of the exit. Check ordinary and boosted travel, relevant nearby craft, supported aspect ratios and frame cost on the exact candidate. Keep simulation-time footage distinct from performance evidence.

Candidate 01 remains the first rejected environment iteration. This report accepts the gallery's structural direction only; it does not accept the finished benchmark, the city composition or motion quality. No art, lighting, camera, gameplay or HUD source was edited for this review.
