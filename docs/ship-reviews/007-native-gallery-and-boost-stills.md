## What is visibly working

The gallery produces a real material response. Entry and middle views tint the pale nacelle shells peach, place a warm narrow highlight on the canopy and show a warmer region on the road. The exit and boost stills return to the cooler exterior appearance. This supports the existence of distinct warm/cool states; smoothness of the transition remains unreviewed.

The track bend and exit are legible, and the ship retains its markings and dark center through both lighting conditions. The route is not hidden behind the gallery walls in these selected camera poses. These are useful improvements to retain while correcting the surrounding construction.

## Current scene failures and bounded prescription

**The gallery reads as black slabs outlined in orange.** Entry/middle fill much of the image with nearly featureless dark side panels, broad black roof areas and bright linear emitters. The depth of wall cassettes, roof returns, structural sections and their connections is largely unreadable. Adding more bright lines will not make these large surfaces feel built.

Correct the existing module kit first. Bring selected structural faces and recessed returns into readable charcoal midtones using restrained material-value separation and broad grazing/wash light. Make the roof panels distinguishable from their supporting ribs and let side bays reveal a few large inset planes. Maintain dark gaps to preserve depth. Reduce strip dominance as the surrounding material becomes visible. Preserve the route, comfortable camera envelope and overall gallery shape unless a specific clearance defect is demonstrated. The next comparison should make those surfaces legible at ordinary chase size.

**The damp-road highlight has an overly regular, hard-edged pattern.** Entry/middle have stronger wet response than the earlier paused exterior, but the broad right-hand streak contains conspicuous repeated diagonal/rectangular boundaries. Those boundaries and the long pale streak compete with the bend and look overly patterned. Break and soften the wetness/light response locally, retain darker interruptions and a quieter central line, and preserve the actual warm pool. The stills do not isolate whether material masks, geometry, lighting or another contribution causes the hard pattern. Diagnose before restoring environment reflections or globally raising gloss.

**The exterior city remains bright window grids over simple dark masses.** The exit/boost shots retain crisp repeated windows across most depth layers while near building service faces reveal little construction. After the gallery repair, concentrate on the few near buildings framing this passage, then lower the far window contrast. This remains the third priority in the [night plan](../night-next-pass-plan.md); a large new tower population is not needed.
