# Independent road review 004 — resumed smoothness comparison

Reviewer: Independent visual reviewer. September 8, 2026. **Final decision: reject candidate 01 as an insufficient visual gain; retain the existing control.** The fresh candidate preserves useful clarity but does not visibly improve the opening passage enough to justify selecting this smoothness change. Do not continue with another roughness variant. Proceed to the bounded reflection-technique feasibility comparison specified in the plan. This decision does not complete the road-production benchmark or award continuous-motion acceptance.

## Original candidate gate, recorded before capture

The candidate must show a useful full-frame improvement over these originals without introducing isolated glossy patches, hard diagonal highlight boundaries, lost joints, weaker vehicle grounding or reduced thermal/station contrast. The gallery-turn view is a particularly useful stress case because its banking and radial panel layout already expose low-contrast boundaries. The station view is useful because the control already has a broad readable light pool; brighter alone would not necessarily improve it.

No candidate images were inspected when this baseline entry was written. No retain/reject decision is assigned in advance. I have not continuously watched the control clip. Still-image inspection, ordered samples, a completed capture and successful video decoding cannot establish highlight stability, smooth light transitions, speed feel or performance. No continuous-motion, manual-play, complete-road or overall-game pass is awarded here.

## Completed candidate comparison

Directly inspected all eight original selected PNGs from the fresh [candidate-01 capture](../../evidence/night-production/road-response/candidate-01/capture-validation.json), native GUID `1441c9f070434c73a2c65dd244581929`. The supplied validation records all 1,440 frames at 1920 × 1080 with successful PNG CRC/decompression checks and unchanged 197 source/resource/settings and 189 app hashes. The earlier resized attempt is preserved separately as `candidate-01-resized` and is excluded from this verdict. These findings concern the fresh complete run only.

The [native material report](../../evidence/night-production/road-response/candidate-01/full-lap/environment-evidence.json) confirms `Universal Render Pipeline/Lit`, `_ENVIRONMENTREFLECTIONS_OFF`, `_METALLICSPECGLOSSMAP` and `_NORMALMAP`; environment reflections are zero, bump scale is 0.25 and the smoothness multiplier is `0.899999976`. The uploaded base-level mask actually spans alpha `124/255–144/255`, approximately `0.486275–0.564706`. Effective measured smoothness is therefore approximately **0.437647–0.508235**. The nominal `.45–.60` generation endpoints, and hence nominal `.405–.54` effective endpoints, are not reached by the generated noise field. The intervention is confirmed, but the nominal range must not be reported as a measured native range. The control's `.315–.45` figure above is likewise its nominal design range; this review does not claim a separately measured control GPU range.

All eight candidate selections fail exact-pose tolerance against the actual fresh control. The five primary camera-position deltas are approximately 0.212–0.327 m; thermal deltas are 1.708 m and 1.714 m; station delta is 2.328 m. The associated rotations/FOV and race times also differ slightly. These remain useful full-frame route comparisons, but fine brightness differences or changed seam positions cannot all be attributed to smoothness. No aligned image subtraction or pixel-matched claim is used.

**The failure is insufficient benefit, rather than a demonstrated severe artifact.** Panel visibility, cyan route edges, craft readability, thermal contrast and station separation remain useful. No obvious return of a high-contrast L-shaped band or destructive diagonal sheen is visible in these eight candidate stills. However, preserving those qualities is not the stated experiment's entire goal. Across the five primary views, the road remains similarly subdued and broad, with little additional light-source character. The measured smoothness change does not establish the useful full-frame gain required to select it.

Retain the control and preserve this failed candidate and its measured readback. The bounded next decision is a feasibility comparison for a different reflection treatment, using the earlier rejected probe evidence and actual native shader/support/cost constraints. This review does not select or implement a reflection technique and does not authorize an unbounded rendering rebuild. It also does not request another mask range: this one-pass material gate has a negative result.

I inspected the sixteen selected control/candidate originals as stills, not continuous playback, and did not inspect additional neighboring frames for this verdict. No assertion is made about temporal highlight drift, banking stability, flicker, transition smoothness, manual control or real-time performance. The missing continuous-motion acceptance is recorded separately from the negative still-image result; additional playback would not turn the absent visible gain in these views into an already established one.
