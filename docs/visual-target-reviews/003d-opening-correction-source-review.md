## Frozen surface review

No blocking source defect found in frozen surface commit `eb453a7`: `OpeningRoadFinish`, its WorldBuilder callsite, the NightTrackLighting correction, dedicated Resources material and integrator's Editor retention guard. Checked actual `_DETAIL_MULX2` implementation, not the earlier `_DETAIL_SCALED` proposal.

- The original road albedo and normal textures are borrowed directly and remain owned by WorldBuilder. The helper owns only its regional meshes and generated macro textures; no new copied fine texture or double-destruction path was introduced.
- Installed URP 17.6 `LitInput.hlsl` applies `_DetailAlbedoMap_ST` to the transformed main UV, then uses that coordinate for both detail maps. Main ST is identity here. The opening scale/offset `(3,54.25)/(0,27.5)` and warm scale/offset `(3,10.25)/(0,206.25)` recover original `(3u,240progress)` with matching phase and orientation. Road positions, collision mesh, triangle partition and copied tangents remain unchanged.
- `_DETAIL_MULX2` computes a factor of twice the sampled detail albedo. A linear RGBAHalf macro-base value .5 therefore cancels that factor, retaining the original sRGB texture's shader-decoded colour at the regional endpoint. Half precision represents .5 exactly. This avoids an extra gamma conversion or an accidental overall albedo doubling. Interior broad modulation only darkens relative to that factor.
- The original runtime RGB-encoded normal with alpha1 is valid for the installed desktop RG/AG unpack branch: the alpha/red multiplication reduces to red, and red/green supply XY. The .25 detail-normal scale matches the control normal scale. The macro normal fades to flat, so RNM blending does not apply the control normal twice at the boundary. Installed `Packing.hlsl` and `CommonMaterial.hlsl` support this reading. Minor floating-point, filtering and normalization differences remain possible.
- Regional macro maps now use 256×1024 and 256×256 textures, with 8-byte half albedo, 8-byte half normal and 4-byte mask texels. The reported approximately 8.33 MiB mip storage follows from those formats, compared with the prior approximately 20 MiB. It excludes driver overhead and does not offset or measure the extra detail texture samples and RNM shader work.
- The original RoadSurface asset remains unchanged. The dedicated Resources material and runtime agree on `_DETAIL_MULX2`, `_NORMALMAP`, `_METALLICSPECGLOSSMAP` and the environment-reflection opt-out. Off still avoids the regional helper. Smoothness endpoints/multiplier, existing light counts, intensity arrays, fog and the exterior caster exclusions remain fixed. Cone/aim changes are bounded to the already-scoped sources; gallery backing tone changes preserve the existing geometry and cool-gallery path.

## Frozen construction review

No remaining blocking source defect found. The original .200/.275 foot lookup, actual socket transform, two contact-building exemptions and preview gates remain intact. The correction raises compact rooms onto the existing 12.75/22 m service datums instead of vertically stretching an empty ground-level recess. Four grounded columns per room, bearing caps, intermittent span supports and continuous longitudinal girders provide actual support relationships. No racing colliders or global scene changes were added.

The source review identified a concrete mapped-surface hazard during implementation: full-length walk layers and podium layers overlapped on the same plane while using different local UV origins. The frozen recipe resolves it by splitting concrete walks/curbs at `room ± 12.9 m` and dark skins at `room ± 12.6 m`, exactly matching the respective 25.8/25.2 m podium footprints. Each resulting span is positive-length in both fixed groups. The continuous girders remain intentional structural contacts. The final roof bottom is 7.6 m above the datum, meeting the wall tops; its top remains 8.5 m above the datum.

Every emitted mesh box is now in the preflight list, including eight rotated housing/lens boxes. Rendering uses the same `q * piece.rotation` transform as world-bounds construction. Landmark, transit and city-footprint checks use conservative group-local bounds for rotated fixtures; full-course clearance uses their complete world AABBs. Both group bounds are accumulated before any acceptance loop can fail. A rejected group still creates neither its meshes nor its lights.

Source counts agree: 49 court boxes + 52 return boxes = 101 boxes / 1,212 triangles, including all eight housing/lens boxes. The four spotlight components remain shadowless and exclude racer layer 8. Their revised intensities total 1,660; this is a reduced construction-light budget, separate from the unchanged surface-light intensity totals. The new floor/support light arrangement is plausible source motivation, not evidence that shadowless light does not leak through intervening geometry.

Metric box UVs use local face coordinates divided by four. All correction boxes opt into those UVs, and the group flush boundaries avoid mixing these vertices with earlier untextured city batches. Off-mode boxes retain their original default path, with no UV assignment. `NightArchitectureFinishes.Apply` supplies existing normal-map-free base/metallic-smoothness maps; no missing-tangent normal-map contract is introduced. The occupied-pane material maps its 4.8 × 3.2 m front from centred metric UVs into a single 0–1 room image. The four new material instances enter `ownMaterials`; generated meshes enter the existing mesh list, while map references retain their original resource/owner lifetime.

The updated numerical audit reports all 101 emitted boxes, minimum protected-road gaps of approximately 22.475/18.762 m, and no conflicts in its conservative 59-site screening.
