# Opening finish draft — independent native whole-frame review

**Verdict: reject the current combined draft as the delivered opening finish.** It makes a visible construction addition at .15, but does not make the district convincingly connected; .22 shows mainly a bright roof wedge. Road response is flatter in the changed opening views and, most clearly, in the warm gallery. This is a useful failed placement/material experiment, not the obvious whole-frame gain required by the [approved approach](002-improvement-approach.md).

## Evidence and limits

I inspected all 18 original 1920 × 1080 PNGs in the same-binary [off selection](../../evidence/nocturne-v2/opening-01/draft/off/selected/) and [combined selection](../../evidence/nocturne-v2/opening-01/draft/combined/selected/): opening01–05, thermal01–02, warm-gallery, and station. Images were opened individually at original detail, without crops, pixel edits, relighting, or difference scoring. I also inspected generated [target A](../../references/nocturne-v2/A-opening-city.png) and [target B](../../references/nocturne-v2/B-amber-gallery.png), following their [reference contract](../../references/nocturne-v2/README.md).

The supplied [off validation](../../evidence/nocturne-v2/opening-01/draft/off/capture-validation.json) and [combined validation](../../evidence/nocturne-v2/opening-01/draft/combined/capture-validation.json) report nine validated PNGs each, native completion, unchanged source/app files during capture, and common build GUID `117ef07dd95f4ceab9efba1e41f5a6d7`. These are natural-threshold diagnostic stills with normal racing camera/physics, not a watched real-time passage. The manifests retain pose differences: combined camera differences from the referenced baseline are about 0.106–0.222 m in the opening/gallery/station and about 1.87 m at the two thermal anchors. Every combined selection fails the manifest's exact-pose tolerance. Small edge, pool, shadow, and framing changes therefore cannot be treated as isolated art effects. No motion, GPU, frame-time, sound, handling, or full-circuit acceptance is claimed.

For historical preservation I additionally inspected the original control [frame 149](../../evidence/night-production/road-response/control/selected/frame-0149.png) and [warm frame 943](../../evidence/night-production/road-response/control/full-lap/03-middle.png). Current off broadly preserves those two compositions, lighting hierarchies, and material appearances. This is a two-view visual spot check against the original app, not independent nine-view historical equivalence. The regression verdicts below compare current combined with current off; they must not be represented as a complete original-control preservation audit.

Target A's useful lesson is the chain of visible relationships: elevated deck into support, support into a podium/service level, then readable adjacent frontage. It also gives the road broad illuminated intervals separated by dark material. The draft exposes pale surfaces but does not yet establish that chain in both required views. Its additions occupy the frame margin while the central road and under-track middle ground retain the original incompleteness. Adding more of the current slab language would amplify the blockout impression.

Target B establishes wall faces, ribs/returns, and road as different materials within one source rhythm. Darkening the ribs moves one part in the right direction. Flattening the much larger road area moves the complete image away from that relationship. The target's fine wet grain is not a literal requirement; losing existing visible response is still a failure without demanding that grain or generated reflections.

## One prioritized correction prescription

**Recompose one readable service bay and its supported connection across .15 and .22, while restoring road response as the correction's preservation condition.** Treat this as one bounded revision of the current passage, not asset multiplication or a new global lighting pass.

1. Make the same compact frontage, return, and support contact visible above the near rail in both native views. Reduce the wide pale roof's dominance, give it a darker top and a readable edge/underside, and direct the practical light toward an occupied recess and a structural return. Carry the support/base connection into visible midtone so the bay cannot read as floating in black space. Preserve open gaps and the mast. A layout that only exposes a roof at .22 must be rejected before adding detail.
2. Recover the control road's visible spatial response before layering broad variation. The acceptance image must retain dark panels and readable seams while showing coherent illuminated intervals along the approach/bend. In the gallery, retain rib separation but remove the dominant faceted wall patches and restore broad broken deck response. This does not require a blanket smoothness increase, painted highlights, or a claim of reflected scene structure.
3. Re-capture the same native nine anchors and require obvious connected frontage at both .15/.22, preserved .30 depth, and a gallery road that is at least as materially legible as off. Evaluate construction/surface component images for attribution, but judge the combined whole image for adoption. Stop this correction if the visible result remains merely a brighter slab plus flatter road.

The integrator relayed proposed compact upper-room placement and a separate detail-normal retention approach while this review was underway. Those hypotheses address the observed visibility and response failures, but I have not reviewed their source or rendered result; this report approves neither dimensions nor implementation. Sources were not edited, and Unity was not run for this review.
