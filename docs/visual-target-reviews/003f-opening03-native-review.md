# Opening03 — independent native whole-frame review

**Decision: reject adoption of the combined surface package; retain the revised construction for a construction-only comparison.** The two opening views now contain readable occupied frontage, and .30 retains depth. Those are useful gains. The road treatment adds obvious broad patches, but the patches do not make the road convincingly respond to its surroundings. In the warm gallery it reduces material legibility relative to off. Current combined should not proceed as the selected finish candidate into full motion/performance acceptance.

This differs from the first draft's verdict: the construction visibility problem has been meaningfully improved. It does not follow that the combined package or complete opening milestone passes. A construction-only selection would preserve a bounded architectural gain while leaving the road/light finish goal explicitly unfinished.

## Adoption recommendation

Keep the revised construction available and evaluate same-binary **construction-only** against off before selecting it. That comparison must confirm that the occupied frontage gain remains while the control road/gallery response is retained, and it should check whether the darker signal framing belongs to the construction state. It is a verification of the bounded architectural selection, not approval to describe the whole road/light finish milestone as complete.

Park the present surface package. Restoring a fine-detail normal or its UV frequency has not recovered the overall material response: the residual failure is plainly visible in the corrected gallery. The integrator's suggested broad roughness/wear-map explanation is plausible, but these images cannot identify the responsible input. Any later diagnosis should compare the original spatial surface response with the new macro treatment under the same sources. Another unmeasured smoothness/intensity sweep is not justified by this evidence.

If construction-only passes the native static check, motion and performance work can assess that selected state. Current combined remains a preserved rejected candidate. Sources were not edited, and Unity was not run for this review.
