# Midnight urban circuit

Create a restrained, industrial night race. Near-black blue city volumes, cool white/cyan road lights, selected warm windows and amber service lights establish depth. The pearl craft should show lit body planes, dark open structure and compact vivid engines. Broad darkness must still reveal believable infrastructure, not simply obscure unfinished surfaces.

The active setting uses constructed ground and architecture instead of exposed ocean and cliffs. A few light corridors and tunnel-like service structures frame speed without placing geometry across the chase camera. Damp road sheen comes from local lights; the previous static environment-reflection artifact must not return. Neon is concentrated on navigation edges and signs, with clear dark intervals.

Review from actual racing camera: readable next bend; clear ship silhouette; body material distinct from engine glow; illuminated road pools; layered city silhouettes/window scales; no giant empty bright slabs, blown-out tubes, missing shaders or reflective cells. Judge moving footage for parallax, lighting rhythm and distracting repeated geometry. Retain independent failures and compare matched views through multiple iterations.
