# Nocturne production authoring

This candidate uses the owner's authorized direct Unity authoring path. The editable source recipe is `UnityProject/Assets/Editor/Production/ProductionArchitecture.cs` and `ProductionGeometry.cs`. Its persisted mesh/material exports are checked in under `UnityProject/Assets/World/NocturneProduction/`, and placements/lights are in `UnityProject/Assets/Scenes/NocturneProduction.unity`.

The previous Sol/Astra immutable Blender-package contract was not implemented. No source blend, texture sheet or artist acceptance is implied by this directory. `asset-ledger.csv` distinguishes retained, replaced, reworked, demoted and unfinished families. The new scene is a functioning candidate, not a finished-game visual pass.

Exact sampled course data is exported from `TrackPath.Evaluate`, not approximated from images; see `evidence/nocturne-production/direct-02/context/`. Architecture retains four intended zones: viaduct 0–.30; canyon .30–.52; thermal .52–.72; station .72–1.00. Saved gallery spans are .35–.435 and .79–.88. Every visual/clearance/finish claim remains subject to native inspection.

Procedural score and sound effects are original mathematical synthesis in `RaceAudio.cs`, using sine harmonics, seeded noise and fixed envelopes; no external recording or commercial music was used. The score is a 16-beat, 112 BPM minor pulse. Signal presence checks do not constitute subjective mix approval.
