Visible progress

Buildings now have structural depth, floor bands, mechanical belts, rooftop forms and broad quiet glass sections. The exit has lower city streets and a skyline on both sides. Window illumination has a floor-level organization instead of uniform scattered squares.

Remaining artistic gap

The result is still more stylized and repetitive than the concepts. Roof equipment and interiors remain simplified; glass reflections are restrained and do not yet reproduce the concepts’ full surface richness or cinematic lighting. This pass is ready for judgment, not a claim of a complete concept match.
